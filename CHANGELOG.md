# DexTrail release history

## 1.0.6 — October 30, 2026

- Fixed repeated artwork warnings after successful Champions updates.
- Missing source images no longer ask you to retry. Available artwork stays in place, and DexTrail checks for the missing images again later.
- Temporary connection problems still show a clear message, while verified Champions information and your saved data stay available.

## 1.0.5 — October 30, 2026

- App update alerts now work on mobile data as well as Wi-Fi.
- See what changed directly in the update prompt.
- Download updates inside DexTrail, follow their progress, and open Android’s installer when they’re ready. Your saved data stays in place.
- Wi-Fi only still applies to PokéAPI and Champions reference downloads. APK downloads start only when you choose.

## 1.0.4 — October 30, 2026

- Added optional usage statistics to help improve DexTrail and make updates more reliable.
- Choose whether to share when you first open this update, or change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy.
- Updated the privacy information to explain what is shared. Your trainer profile, teams and collections stay on your device.

## 1.0.3 — October 30, 2026

- Fixed Champions updates failing when a guide illustration is unavailable.
- Rules, eligible Pokémon and guide information can now refresh without being blocked by an optional image.
- Available saved artwork stays in place, with a clear message when an image needs another attempt.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-10-29

Champions updates, without losing your place

- Fixed the Champions update button jumping back to the top of the page.
- Added live progress for announcements, eligible Pokémon, guide sections, artwork and saving changes.
- You can pause and resume a Champions check without losing your place. Interrupted checks keep your saved information safe.
- Kept roster searches and expanded details in place when Champions information refreshes.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-29

Smoother PokéAPI updates

- Fixed PokéAPI updates getting stuck on “Checking for updates · 1 of 5.”
- Checks now use PokéAPI’s official data files and reuse unchanged downloads to keep repeat checks fast.
- Added clearer progress and connection messages. Pausing an update responds promptly, even if the connection stalls.
- Incomplete checks keep your existing data safe and can be retried.
- Updated the privacy-policy link to the DexTrail website.
