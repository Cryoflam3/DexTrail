# DexTrail

Your Pokémon companion for Android. Look up Pokémon, explore regional maps, build teams, and keep track of your collection in one place.

[Visit the DexTrail website](https://cryoflam3.github.io/DexTrail/) · [Privacy policy](https://cryoflam3.github.io/DexTrail/privacy.html)

## Download

Get the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/Cryoflam3/DexTrail/releases/latest).

**Version 1.0.5 · Android 5.1 or newer**

1. Download `DexTrail-1.0.5.apk` from the release.
2. Open the file on your Android device. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager.
3. Choose a nickname and avatar, set your preferences, and start exploring.

DexTrail checks for new app releases when you open it, using Wi-Fi or mobile data. The prompt shows the release notes and lets you choose **Download update** or **Later**. Downloads happen inside DexTrail, with progress shown before Android asks you to confirm installation. The Wi-Fi-only setting still applies to PokéAPI and Champions reference updates.

For future updates, install the new APK over your existing copy. Export a saved-data backup first so you have an extra copy of your records.

## What’s inside

- **Pokédex:** Pokémon and forms, artwork and sprites, stats, moves, abilities, and type matchups.
- **Maps and locations:** Regional maps, encounter information, and Legends: Z-A locations, including Lumiose City and Hyperspace areas.
- **Team tools:** Build and save teams, compare Pokémon, and explore moves and types.
- **Your collection:** Track caught Pokémon, favorites, and shiny hunts.
- **Reference guides:** Trainers, items, ribbons, game versions, and event Pokémon.
- **Champions:** Browse the guide, roster, and regulations, and check for updated information.
- **PokéAPI updates:** Refresh supported reference data and review the latest changes.
- **Personal touches:** Trainer avatars, Light, Dark, and AMOLED themes, and selectable app icons.

The bundled reference content works offline. Online updates and external source links need an internet connection. No online account is required; your profile and saved records stay on your device.
