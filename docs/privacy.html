DexTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how DexTrail handles information. It covers the Android app with package name com.dextrail.community. For privacy questions or requests, contact DexTrail.Feedback@proton.me.

Your profile and saved data

DexTrail does not require an online account. The name and avatar you choose create a profile on your device. You can use a nickname; you do not need to provide your real name. Your profile, preferences, favorites, caught Pokémon, teams, builds, and shiny-hunt records are stored in the app's private storage. DexTrail does not upload these records to a developer-operated server.

DexTrail does not request your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Internet connections and updates

The bundled reference content works offline. When connected, DexTrail checks for reference updates and can download data, artwork, and audio directly from public sources. These include PokéAPI and its official data files hosted on GitHub, official Pokémon websites and their content servers, Serebii, Bulbagarden, and Pokémon Showdown, depending on the feature used.

These connections necessarily disclose your IP address to the receiving service, together with the requested resource, request time, and an app-identifying request header. Requests may also include technical cache information to avoid downloading unchanged content. DexTrail does not attach your profile, saved teams, collections, or advertising identifier to these requests. The receiving providers handle connection information under their own policies; DexTrail does not control their server logs or retention.

PokéAPI and Champions checks can run while the app is open, normally no more than once every six hours after a successful check. PokéAPI changes are applied when you request an update. Champions automatic updates are off by default; enabling them also allows scheduled background updates. Turning that option off stops automatic application of Champions updates, but does not disable foreground checks for new information. Wi-Fi-only restrictions apply to these connections. You can use the bundled content without a network connection.

When you open DexTrail, it also checks for newer app releases on GitHub. This shares the connection information described above with GitHub, respects the Wi-Fi-only update setting, and does not send your saved records. If a newer APK is available, you can choose Update now or Later. Android asks you to confirm installation.

Files, sharing, and external links

After profile setup, DexTrail also checks GitHub for a newer APK when you open the app. The check follows your Wi-Fi-only preference. It sends a request for the latest public DexTrail release, with normal connection information and an app-identifying header, but no profile or saved records. A small copy of the release response may be cached on your device. If a newer APK is available, you can open its GitHub download page or continue using the app. DexTrail does not automatically install updates.

Backup export and import use Android's file picker. Exported backups can contain your profile, preferences, and saved records. Import restores supported collection and team records. Backups are not password-protected by DexTrail, so store them somewhere you trust. Choosing a cloud storage destination may transfer the file to that provider.

If you choose to share an image, Android sends the selected image to the app you choose. Copying a team places its text on your clipboard. External source links open outside DexTrail and are subject to the destination's privacy practices. These actions occur only when you choose them.

Support messages

The Feedback button opens a draft in your email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in the message or attachments. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal details. Email is handled by our email provider, Proton. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements; you may request deletion by contacting the address above.

Retention and deletion

Your local profile and saved records remain on your device until you change or remove them, clear DexTrail's app storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Cached reference content may be replaced as updates are downloaded. The app may keep a local recovery backup before importing saved data; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes these private recovery copies too.

Exported files are outside the app's private storage and must be deleted separately from the folder or cloud service you selected. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete exported backups. Because we do not receive your local profile or saved records, we cannot recover or remotely delete them for you. There is no online DexTrail account to close.

Children's privacy

DexTrail is intended for a general audience, including children. A nickname and a built-in avatar are sufficient to use it. The app does not upload children's profiles or saved records and does not use behavioral advertising. Online reference requests still disclose the technical connection information described above. Parents and guardians can help children use the app offline, manage exported files, and contact us with privacy concerns. If a child sends personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian may request its deletion.

Security and your choices

Reference downloads use HTTPS. App-private data is protected by Android's application storage controls. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. You control whether to export or share files, open external links, enable Champions automatic updates, or remove local data.

DexTrail does not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of information you have sent to us. Contact us to make a request; do not send identity documents unless we explain why verification is necessary. Information held by an independent source website is subject to that provider's processes.

This website and GitHub downloads

This website stores your Light or Dark theme choice in your browser. It does not add advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider receives normal connection information needed to serve the page. Downloads and repository links open GitHub, which handles visitors’ information under its own privacy policy.

Policy changes

We will update this policy when DexTrail's data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will also be explained in the app or its release information where appropriate.
